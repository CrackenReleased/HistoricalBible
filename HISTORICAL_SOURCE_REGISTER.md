# Historical source register and artifact review queue

Reviewed 2026-10-20. This is an editorial status register, not a claim that every existing atlas sentence is verified. All 20 entries in `src/data/ancientMapData.ts` now have individually scoped receipts. `attested` describes what an object directly attests; `inferred`, `proposed`, and `disputed` mark the narrower passage connection or identification. A link alone, including a museum page, does not promote a card to reviewed. No third-party image, full inscription transcription, or lexicon text was copied in this revision.

## Review rule

An artifact can be promoted only after a reviewer records an object-specific primary collection or edition ID, the exact claim supported by it, passage connection method, dating basis, limitation, place precision, and rights for any reused media. Add a typed `EvidenceReceipt` in `src/data/evidenceReceipts.ts` and a focused regression. `getArtifactDisplay` then uses the reviewed copy. Disputed identities must say disputed. A record that names a ruler does not by itself prove every event in the associated passage.

## Active limits

- Twenty artifact records and two passage-specific dates are reviewed here. Several identifications and interpretations remain explicitly proposed or disputed; their receipts document the limits. These are not twenty proven synchronies.
- Coordinates are existing approximate atlas points; this revision does not validate exact excavation positions.
- Only three explicit KJV quotation links and one Hebrew occurrence alignment have been individually checked. This is a seed, not a completed concordance or global chronology.
