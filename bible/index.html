TEXTUAL FIDELITY & NOTATION

What makes this edition distinct

Our interactive reader and downloadable PDF share the exact same canonical text conventions:

Explicit {chapter:verse} Markers

Every verse is anchored with clear {c:v} delimiters (such as LUKE {3:1}) for rapid verification and cross-referencing.

Bracketed [...] Translator Words

Words supplied by the King James translators for English grammar are preserved transparently inside brackets and rendered in italics in the study desk.

Preserved LORD Typography

The covenant name of God is faithfully distinguished in small-caps typography (LORD) throughout the Old Testament.
