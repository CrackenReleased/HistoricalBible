PURPOSE

Why we are here

What else was happening in the world during the events of the Bible? Historical Bible helps you explore the people, kingdoms, places and events of Scripture alongside the wider history of their time. Timelines, maps and historical records support that exploration, with biblical testimony, interpretation and external evidence clearly distinguished.

CONVICTION

What we believe

We at Historical Bible believe that the Bible is the inerrant word of God. We believe the Bible literally, and that it is not only historically accurate, but that it is also eschatologically accurate.

Why the KJV A brief note for the curious

Unchanging Standard: No modern copyright restrictions, no shifting editions every few years, and no editorial footnotes casting doubt on whether God’s Word belongs in the text.

Doctrinal & Textual Faithfulness: The KJV does not water down the deity of Jesus Christ, the blood atonement, repentance, or salvation by grace through faith.

Translator Honesty: Unlike modern paraphrases that blend human interpretation into the verse, the KJV openly shows its work—distinguishing God’s covenant name LORD for Jehovah and marking every translator-supplied word in brackets so you always know what is directly in the underlying text.
