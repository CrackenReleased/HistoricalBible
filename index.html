Why we are here

Are you struggling to correlate the people's and timelines of the Bible to your known world history? Do you feel like the historical events recorded in the Bible are separate from world history? At Historical Bible we endeavor to connect those dots and unblur those lines. This is where history, faith and religion come together.

What we believe

We at Historical Bible believe that the Bible is the inerrant word of God. We believe the Bible literally, and that it is not only historically accurate, but that it is also eschatologically accurate.
